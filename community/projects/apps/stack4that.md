## When to use

- Turn a product brief into a proposed stack without hand-authoring every choice.
- Mismatch: Architecture suggestions are not a substitute for production review.

The app stores your TypeSafe key and runs decision contracts that select stack pieces. (Summarized from the upstream [README](https://github.com/MoRohn/stack4that/blob/33836e63ee9fe2e381774a3000d9f2745677f92e/README.md); not reproduced here.)
