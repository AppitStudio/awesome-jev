## When to use

Use when paste a résumé or backstory and get a 1920s TRPG investigator sheet judged by TypeSafe Jev (free live site; nothing stored). Prefer other catalog trading/game tools when you need paper-trading or non-Jev judges.

## How it works

Jev answers multiple-choice and scoring questions for occupation, characteristics, and skills; app lays out the sheet. Application code owns orchestration and side effects beyond the judgment.
