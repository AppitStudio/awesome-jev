## When to use

- Work through book labs with runnable Jev clients.
- Mismatch: Teaching code—not a turnkey product.

## How it works

Each mini-project posts typed questions via shared Jev client helpers. Evidence: [`capstone-project/src/triage/jev_client.py`](https://github.com/udooz/mastering-jev-and-kev-book/blob/54f57566b3f978f47d679a8d052f77f1b9a89815/capstone-project/src/triage/jev_client.py).
