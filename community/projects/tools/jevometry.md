## When to use

Use when studying sensitivity/geometry of typed decision probability outputs. Prefer SDKs/routers when you need to call Jev in production.

## How it works

Takes declared System One–style probability models and applies Fisher information / sensitivity analysis; includes an offline three-agent cleaning example.
